# THURSDAY — design (beta)

A Call of Duty-style shooter set in an alternate, compressed Rancho Cucamonga around the year 2000, with the fish-out-of-water comedy
of the early-2000s suburb movies: a guy from the city lands in the suburbs, and the neighborhood's loudest characters end up being the
ones who save it. Built on the Operación Insurgentes engine (same tech, controls, phone support, multiplayer).

## Ground rules (keep these true)

- **Original work.** Inspired by the feel of those movies, never copied from them: no character from any film, no lookalikes or
  sound-alike names, no lines, scenes, title, logos or music. The cast below is our own.
- **No real people.** No real person's likeness or name in the game.
- **No stereotypes.** Characters are funny because of who they are (bossy, cocky, lazy, paranoid), never because of race or class
  caricature. The enemy is a corporate private army, not a gang or any ethnic group.
- **Real places, fictional businesses.** Real main streets and the city's shape are used. Big landmarks are recognisable by layout and
  silhouette but carry fictional names (the GTA approach), so no shopping center, mall or business is named or branded.
- **Residential streets are made up**, modelled on the generic look of a 1990s Rancho tract, not on anyone's real street or house.
  Nobody's home address is used or marked.
- Schools are scenery. No shooting in or targeting of school grounds; no spawns there.
- Civilians are not targets. Hurting them is penalised, as in the hometown game.

## Premise

It's Thursday, the day before the weekend. Ray just moved out from the city to stay with his aunt in Rancho Cucamonga for a fresh start.
That morning **Kingsley Prime**, a developer that wants to bulldoze half of Rancho for a luxury "lifestyle district", sends its private
security army in to "secure the area": black polos, khaki pants, tactical vests, golf-cart patrols, a command post in the big outlet mall.
The HOA is outraged. Ray is just trying to get through the day.

## Cast (original)

- **Ray** — the player. From the city, unimpressed by everything, dry one-liners.
- **Aunt Ro (Rochelle)** — HOA president, runs the fight over a walkie-talkie like a general. Takes lawn violations and invasions equally
  seriously. Main radio voice (the "COMMAND" role).
- **Junebug** — Ray's cousin, works a kiosk at the outlet mall, always mid-hustle. AI squadmate, talks too much.
- **Mr. Pennington** — retired neighbour, obsessed with his lawn, turns out to be a very good shot. AI squadmate / overwatch.
- **Bobo** — the block's legendary giant: a huge, ponytailed ex-pro-wrestler in flip-flops and a too-small souvenir T-shirt who
  rides a tiny scooter and adores his little dog, Princess. Everyone on the block is scared of him. He is in a terrible mood because
  Kingsley's goons towed his car. First he is a boss fight (he thinks Ray is one of them: big health bar, slow, charges and slams),
  then a reluctant ally for the finale. His own look and attitude; he is not modelled on any film character.
- **Chet Kingsley** — the developer. Heard on loudspeakers and radio intercepts, never fought face to face until the end.

## Map (compressed, about 30% of real distances; houses and buildings at real size)

Real arterials on the real grid: Archibald, Haven, Milliken, Day Creek (north–south); Base Line, Church, Foothill Blvd (Route 66),
4th St (east–west); the 15 freeway on the east side; the Metrolink tracks. San Gabriel Mountains fill the northern horizon.

Landmarks (fictional names, recognisable shapes):
- **Terra Linda tract** (made up): the starting neighbourhood north of Foothill — two-story stucco houses, tile roofs, garages,
  lawns, block walls, palms, cul-de-sacs.
- **Foothill Blvd strip** — Route 66 strip malls, gas stations, fast-food pads, parking lots.
- **"The Market Hall"** on Haven south of Foothill — a food hall in a big barn-like building with an outdoor patio and stage.
- **Civic center** by Haven — city hall plaza and fountains.
- **"Rancho Promenade"** at Foothill and Day Creek — an outdoor main-street mall: a street of storefronts, a fountain plaza, a
  little train.
- **The ballpark** — a minor-league stadium by the Metrolink station.
- **"Inland Empire Outlets"** by the 15 freeway in the south — a giant single-story outlet mall ringed by parking lots; Kingsley's
  command post and the finale.

## Mission (beta: 7 stages)

1. **Wake-up call** — Aunt Ro's house in the tract. Clear the security patrol off the street.
2. **Cul-de-sac standoff** — hold the cul-de-sac while neighbours get out.
3. **Route 66** — push down to Foothill, clear the strip mall and the gas station.
4. **The Market Hall** — retake the food hall on Haven.
5. **Bobo** — Bobo blocks the way at the civic center; knock him down without killing him (he has a big health bar and charges).
   He joins you.
6. **The Promenade** — fight down the outdoor mall's main street to the fountain.
7. **The Outlets** — storm the outlet mall parking lots and take Kingsley's command post.

Survival and multiplayer (Versus arenas: the tract, the Promenade, the Outlets parking lot; co-op mission) carry over from the engine.

## Look

Late-afternoon Inland Empire light, a little smog haze, the mountains sharp and huge to the north; around 2000: older cars (sedans,
pickups, minivans, a lowrider or two), satellite dishes, basketball hoops over garages, sprinklers, Santa Ana wind dust.

## Additions from the owner's friend (beta)

- **The Pacific Electric Trail** (real public rail-trail, keeps its real name) runs east–west just north of Base Line (game z ≈ −636),
  from Archibald past Haven to Central Park. Stage 5 is a chase along it: Ray and Junebug on lowrider bikes, Bobo after them on his
  tiny scooter. The Bobo fight moves to the open lawn of Central Park (Milliken & Base Line).
- **Mission is now 8 stages:** 1 wake-up call, 2 cul-de-sac, 3 Route 66, 4 Market Hall, 5 the Trail (bike chase), 6 Bobo at
  Central Park, 7 the Promenade, 8 the Outlets. Long gaps between stages are bridged by a short fade ("I know a shortcut").
- **Third-person view by default** (over the shoulder, like GTA), first person as an option; lowrider bikes you can ride.
- **The friend as a playable character, for the beta only.** A basic avatar from two photos he sends (front and back): skin tone,
  hair, clothes and a simple face texture. Only the small game textures made from the photos go in the game and the repository,
  never the original photos; the character is taken out before a public release unless he says otherwise.
