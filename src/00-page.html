    Keyboard and mouse

    WASD move, Shift sprint, C crouch, Space jump. Left click fire, right click aim. R reload, Q swap, G grenade, V knife, F pick up or use (hold F by a bike to ride it), T switch view, M map, Esc pause.

    Phone or tablet

    Left thumb moves, right thumb aims. Push the stick all the way forward to sprint. FIRE can be dragged to keep aiming, and it fires by itself when your sights are on a hostile. VIEW switches the camera; USE by a bike rides it. Turn the phone sideways.
